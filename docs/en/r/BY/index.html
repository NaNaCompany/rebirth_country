BEHIND THE CHANCE

There is a method behind the chance.

Population mode weights 199 countries and passport-issuing territories by their population. More populous places are more likely to be drawn.

These are game probabilities, not actual birth or citizenship probabilities. Equal mode gives every place the same chance.

Excluded places are removed and the remaining weights are normalized. Shared links preview your passport, while visitors start their own game on the home screen.
